# Vintage Slab Finder feed searches (rewritten 28 Oct 2026; tidied into categories 13 Nov 2026).
# One search per line; each runs once per country listed in ebay_search.py.
# Lines starting with "+" are want-list searches: modern (2018+) cards are kept for them.
# Every result still has to have a grade (PSA/CGC/BGS 9-10) or NM/mint in its title to be kept.
# eBay returns at most 200 results per search, so busy sets are split by grade.
# (a,b) means a OR b; -word drops titles with that word.
# "# ---------- Name ----------" lines are the categories the finder and the daily summary group cards by.
# A listing is filed under every category whose searches fit its title, not just the search that found it.
# Keep the "BW / Plasma-era secret rares" heading as it is: the NM alert reads it.
# Options at the end of a line (not searched for):
#   @c=AU,JP   only search these countries (saves 2 calls a day per country left out)
#   @nonm      skip the extra "Near Mint or Better" pass (halves the calls)
#   @pawn      only the pawn-shop sellers (AU)      @trusted   only the sellers in trusted_sellers.txt
# queries_report.md (daily) lists searches that find nothing, and countries that never find anything for a line.
# Removed 19 Nov 2026 (every result was already found by other searches): expedition reverse holo psa, e-series cgc,
#   GL holo psa, prime near mint, and the three named-Prime lines.
